About me

I'm a mechanical engineer who treats software as a tool, not an identity. My real interest sits at the hardware–software intersection — designing physical systems, then making them intelligent through machine learning and reinforcement learning. I like problems that force the math and the metal to agree: state estimation that has to hold up against a real (messy, drifting) physical signal; a thermodynamic cycle that has to close on paper before it means anything in hardware.

That instinct — build the physical thing, then make it smart — is what's pulling me toward robotics research and, eventually, robotics R&D roles in industry. Companies like Amazon Robotics and ABB are on my radar; a PhD remains an open path depending on where the research takes me.

Outside of engineering, I play basketball, cycle, and do calisthenics — currently working on building up core strength.
