Senior Capstone Report

An Extended Kalman Filter Approach to Bio-Inspired Magnetic Signature Navigation Under Secular Variation

Emmanuel Amos · advised by Dr. Brian Taylor
Case Western Reserve University · Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering · 2025–2026

Develops and validates an Extended Kalman Filter for a simulated migratory agent navigating by Earth's magnetic signature (intensity and inclination). Removes the standard assumption of static, perfectly-known field values by modeling and correcting for secular variation — the field's slow drift over time. Validated across 250 migration cycles under translation, rotation, and combined field-change conditions.
